Algebraic Automata Theory #2 - Homomorphisms

2026-09-02

Perhaps the most important feature of modern mathematics is the study of not only structures, but also of the structure preserving maps between them:

Def: Let S=(S,⋅)S = (S, \cdot) and T=(T,×)T = (T, \times) be semigroups. A semigroup homomorphism is a function φ:S→T\varphi : S \to T such that φ(a⋅b)=φ(a)×φ(b)\varphi(a \cdot b) = \varphi(a) \times \varphi(b)

This notion extends easily to monoids by requiring that the identity element is also preserved, i.e., φ(1S)=1T\varphi(1_S) = 1_T.
